Privacy Policy for Pantry Helper

This Privacy Policy explains how Pantry Helper: Food & Grocery (Package: com.ordinarylabs.pantryhelper), developed and published by Ordinary Labs (“we”, “our”, or “us”), handles user information when you use our Android application.

Privacy in Brief:
  • Zero Account Required: We do not operate user accounts, logins, or tracking servers.
  • 100% Local Device Storage: Your grocery lists, pantry inventory, spending notes, and receipt data stay on your phone.
  • Private Receipt Scanner: Text recognition runs entirely on your device via Google ML Kit. Receipt photos and recognized items are never uploaded to our servers.
  • Advertising & Remove Ads Option: Free users may see non-intrusive ads provided by Google AdMob. You can permanently disable all ads at any time via the one-time “Remove Ads (Lifetime)” in-app purchase in Settings.

2. Information You Create and Manage (Local Only)

Pantry Helper functions as a private, on-device assistant. All user-generated content is saved exclusively within your Android device's sandboxed local application storage (Room database, JSON files, and Jetpack DataStore):

  • Shopping Lists: Item names, quantities, unit prices, custom emojis, aisle categories, and completed checkbox states.
  • Pantry Inventory: Stock levels, running-low flags, and staple ingredients.
  • Shopping History & Spend Tracking: Past grocery trip dates, supermarket names, itemized receipts, store discounts/coupons, sales tax totals, and personal trip notes.
  • App Preferences: Selected visual theme (Default Warm Cream, Dark Mode, etc.), sound effect toggles, and notification settings.

Ordinary Labs has zero access to this data. We do not run synchronization servers, cloud databases, or remote telemetry backends for your shopping or pantry data.

3. Device Permissions & Receipt Scanning

Pantry Helper requests only the minimal permissions required to provide its core features:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used strictly on an opt-in basis when you choose the “Take Photo with Camera” option in the Receipt Scanner. The camera feed is used solely to capture a receipt picture. We do not record video, access the camera in the background, or transmit photos externally.
  • Photo Gallery / Photo Picker: You may alternatively select an existing receipt photo using Android's privacy-preserving system Photo Picker (which requires zero broad storage permissions). The selected image is processed locally and never shared.
  • Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): On Android 13+ (API 33+), the app may request permission to display optional reminders (such as low-stock pantry notifications or grocery reminders you configure). You can disable notifications at any time in the app Settings or Android system settings.

4. On-Device Text Recognition (Google ML Kit)

When you scan a grocery receipt, receipt text recognition is performed entirely on your device using Google ML Kit Text Recognition.

  • Local Optical Character Recognition (OCR): Google states that the ML Kit Text Recognition API processes images locally on-device and does not send image pixels or extracted grocery item text to Google servers.
  • SDK Diagnostic Telemetry: To maintain software quality and hardware acceleration, the ML Kit client library may automatically transmit generic diagnostic metrics to Google (such as device manufacturer, Android build, API performance stats, and error codes). This diagnostic data is encrypted in transit and does not contain your receipt photos or list content. For details, review Google ML Kit Terms and the ML Kit Android Data Disclosure.

5. Advertising & Monetization (Google AdMob)

To keep Pantry Helper free for everyone, the free version displays third-party advertisements served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). Ad formats include non-intrusive bottom banners, native shopping list cards, and occasional interstitials during list completion.

Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous information to serve, measure, and protect against fraudulent ad impressions:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID), IP address, network carrier, and device hardware/operating system specifications.
  • Ad Interaction Metrics: Impressions, click data, diagnostics, and app performance telemetry.

For further details on how Google uses information from apps that use its services, visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and Google Privacy Policy.

Complete Ad Removal (“Remove Ads” Lifetime Purchase):

If you prefer not to see ads or have advertising identifiers processed by third-party ad networks, Pantry Helper provides an optional one-time Remove Ads (Lifetime) upgrade. This option is accessible at any time directly in the app under Settings → Remove Ads.

Once purchased, all AdMob banner ads, native list cards, and interstitial popups are permanently disabled. The Google Mobile Ads SDK ceases requesting or loading advertisements on your device, ensuring a completely ad-free, undisturbed experience.

Managing Your Ad Preferences:

  • Reset or Delete Advertising ID: On your Android device, go to Settings → Google → Ads to reset or delete your Advertising ID.
  • Personalized Ads Opt-Out: You can opt out of personalized interest-based advertising through Android system settings. If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will be contextual rather than personalized.

6. European Privacy Rights (GDPR & UK GDPR) and Consent Management

For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom (UK), and Switzerland, we comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF v2.2) via the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK.

  • Consent Choice: When launching the app in applicable regions, you are presented with a consent dialog allowing you to consent to or decline personalized advertising and associated vendor cookies/mobile identifiers.
  • In-App Consent Revocation: You can review, modify, or revoke your consent choices at any time directly inside the app by going to:
    Pantry Helper → Settings → Ad Privacy & Consent Choices.

7. “Remove Ads” In-App Purchase & Google Play Billing

Pantry Helper offers an optional Remove Ads (Lifetime) non-consumable in-app purchase.

  • Secure Payment Processing: All in-app purchase transactions are processed directly and securely through the Google Play In-App Billing system (Google LLC).
  • Zero Financial Data Access: Ordinary Labs never receives, processes, or stores your credit card number, banking details, or billing address. All financial transactions are strictly governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service.
  • Purchase Entitlement & Restoration: The Google Play Billing library communicates with Google Play servers to verify and restore your purchase status. A cryptographic purchase token is retained locally in your device's sandboxed storage to permanently activate ad-free mode. You can restore your purchase at any time across devices signed into the same Google account using the “Restore Purchases” button under Settings.

8. United States State Privacy Laws (CCPA / CPRA & Others)

If you are a resident of California or other US states with comprehensive consumer privacy statutes:

  • No Sale of Personal Data: Ordinary Labs does not sell, rent, or trade your grocery, pantry, receipt, or personal information to third parties.
  • Cross-Context Behavioral Advertising: Third-party advertising partners (Google AdMob) may collect pseudonymous identifiers as described above. You may exercise your right to opt out of interest-based tracking at any time via your device's Android Ad Settings or by purchasing the Ad-Free upgrade.

9. Android Auto Backup & Device Transfers

Android provides built-in system backup capabilities (Android Auto Backup and device-to-device transfers). Depending on your device settings and Google Drive backup configuration, your local grocery lists and pantry database may be included in encrypted device backups managed by your Google account.

These backups are governed by your personal Google account security and Android settings. Ordinary Labs has no independent access to your Google Drive backup archives. You can manage or disable app backups via Android Settings → System → Backup.

10. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your data is stored locally on your device:

  • User-Controlled Deletion: You can delete individual grocery items, pantry staples, or past shopping lists directly within the app at any time.
  • Complete App Wipe: Clearing the app's storage via Android Settings → Apps → Pantry Helper → Storage & Cache → Clear Storage or uninstalling the app permanently deletes all locally stored lists, receipts, and history from your device.
