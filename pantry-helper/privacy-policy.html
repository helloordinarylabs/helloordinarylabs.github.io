Privacy Policy for Pantry Helper: Grocery List

This policy explains how Pantry Helper: Grocery List, published by Ordinary Labs, handles information when you use the Android app.

In brief: Pantry Helper does not require an account. Grocery and pantry content is used by the app on your device. Receipt text recognition is processed on-device by Google ML Kit, but the ML Kit SDK may send separate diagnostic and usage metrics to Google. Android system backup may also copy app data depending on the app and device settings.

1. Developer and contact

Pantry Helper: Grocery List is developed by Ordinary Labs. For privacy questions, contact hello.ordinarylabs@gmail.com.

2. Information you enter or choose to scan

The app may store information you add to use its features, such as pantry item names, quantities and low-stock status; grocery-list items; shopping trip dates and totals; taxes, savings, price notes and store notes; app preferences; and text recognized from receipts.

If you choose to scan a receipt, the app uses the camera or an image you select. Receipt images and recognized text are used to extract candidate items, prices and quantities for your review. You decide what to keep in your lists and trip history.

3. Where this information is processed

Ordinary Labs does not operate a Pantry Helper account service or server that receives your grocery lists, pantry records, receipt images, or OCR results. The app stores its working data in its local app storage and uses it to provide the features described above. The app does not require cloud sync or sign-up.

4. Google ML Kit receipt recognition

Google ML Kit performs text recognition on-device. Google states that ML Kit does not send the input image or recognition result to Google servers. Separately, ML Kit may contact Google for bug fixes, updated models, or hardware compatibility information, and the Android SDK sends API performance and utilization metrics to Google.

Google’s disclosure for ML Kit describes metrics that may include device information (such as manufacturer, model, operating system/build, or available hardware accelerators), app information (such as package name and app version), identifiers used for diagnostics and analytics, and performance or usage metrics. Depending on the ML Kit implementation, an identifier may be device-level or per-installation. Google says this metrics data is encrypted in transit and is not transferred to third parties by ML Kit.

These metrics are separate from receipt content. Ordinary Labs does not receive receipt images or OCR results through ML Kit. Read Google ML Kit Terms & Privacy and Google’s ML Kit Android data disclosure for details.

5. Camera and photo access

Camera access is used when you choose to take a receipt photo. You may instead select an image using Android’s photo picker. Pantry Helper does not need continuous camera access to manage lists or pantry items. The selected image is used for the scan you request.

6. Network access, offline use, and third parties

The grocery-list and pantry features are designed to work without an account or a continuous internet connection. The ML Kit SDK may use a network connection for the separate Google services and metrics described above. Ordinary Labs does not sell your list, pantry, receipt, or shopping-history content, and does not run a separate cloud-sync service for Pantry Helper.

7. Android backups and device transfers

Android provides system backup and device-to-device transfer features. Whether Pantry Helper data is included can depend on the app’s backup configuration, Android version, device manufacturer, and your device settings. If system backup is enabled for the app, Android may copy app data to your Google account or transfer it to another device. These system services are governed by your Android and Google account settings. Learn about Android Auto Backup.

8. Retention and deletion

Pantry and shopping information remains in the app until you remove it or clear the app’s stored data. Use the app’s available controls to delete items or trip records. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app generally removes its local app data; copies made through Android backup or device transfer are subject to those services’ settings and retention practices. Ordinary Labs does not keep a server-side copy of this app content.

9. Children

Pantry Helper is a general-purpose household shopping utility and is not designed to collect information from children. The app does not require an account or ask for a child’s personal details.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated to describe the change. The latest version will be available at this page.

11. Contact us

For questions about this policy or Pantry Helper’s privacy practices, email hello.ordinarylabs@gmail.com.
