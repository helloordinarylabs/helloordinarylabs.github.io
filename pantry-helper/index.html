Pantry Helper: Grocery List

Know what’s at home before you shop. Keep pantry staples, grocery lists, receipt details, and trip spending together in one straightforward app.

Read Pantry Helper’s privacy policy

Everything for the next shop

Keep an eye on staples

Track everyday essentials, mark items running low, and move them to your active shopping list with a tap. Restock after a shopping trip.

Build a useful grocery list

Add items with catalog suggestions, sort by supermarket aisle, set quantities, and cross items off as you shop.

Scan receipts on your device

Use the camera or choose a receipt image. On-device text recognition helps extract item names, prices, and quantities for you to review.

Review your spending

Archive shopping trips with dates, totals, taxes, and savings. Look back at monthly totals, average trip costs, and notes about prices or stores.

Use quick home-screen widgets

Open common actions from a widget or check running-low pantry essentials at a glance.

Make it comfortable

Choose OLED dark mode, adjust text size, and use the app in its available languages.

Designed for local use

No account is required. Receipt text recognition runs on-device; Google ML Kit may still send diagnostic and usage metrics to Google. See the policy for those details and Android backup behavior.
